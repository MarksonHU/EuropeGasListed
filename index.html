Source hierarchy and refresh cadence

The page is now explicit about what sits in the source-of-record layer and what belongs in the context layer.

Official market & macro

Source of record

EEX for European gas benchmarks, ECB for rates and reference FX, Eurostat for HICP, AGSI/GIE for storage. Refresh cadence: same day for markets, monthly for inflation.

Annual reports, earnings presentations, plant footprint and hedging disclosures should anchor company-specific claims and process descriptions.

Newswires & market wrap

Context

Reuters and similar wires are useful for fast-moving Brent, TTF shock context, policy reaction and event chronology, but they should not outrank official data when the latter are available.
